## Honest limitations

- All geometry, colours and positions are authored in uncalibrated design units.
  No metric scale, registration or source connectivity is established; flags
  remain false. The relief is invented, not measured or inferred from the source.
- Agents cannot judge appearance. The headless checks are mechanical assertions
  and the verifier is arithmetic/static; none is approval. A passing validator
  or clean capture is not approval.
- The walk path is a deterministic set of fixed camera poses, not a physics-driven
  walk; actual physics traversal is covered separately by the headless test's
  walk-and-backtrack, which does not render. The operator has the renders and can
  run the scene to walk it.
- The 9 baseline PNGs and its manifest live under `evidence/**/captures/` and stay
  local under the operator `.gitignore` policy; the variant's 9 PNGs are under the
  room's tracked `captures/`. Both are regenerable with commands 6–7. The guard
  receipts, provenance, verifier and candidate record are committed.
- The v2 headless test instantiates v1 beside v2 only to capture its collider
  signature, then removes v1 before ray checks so the two scenes never share the
  physics world during negative controls.
- Scope compliance: no ledger events were appended; no hash, receipt or decision
  was written by hand (all were computed by the new generators or the existing
  guard wrapper). No Hunyuan attempt, model call, download, catalog/snapshot edit,
  `register_source_room.py`, module metadata or integration proposal was made.
