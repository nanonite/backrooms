# Chainlink #200 — material-aware footsteps

**Status:** implementation and verification complete for review. No commit. #200 remains open.

## Implementation

### Walkthrough player

`scripts/walk_player.gd` (the only player script changed) now:

- triggers footsteps by horizontal distance travelled: 0.7 m walking, 1.0 m sprinting;
- raycasts down to the floor collider and reads `surface_acoustic`;
- loads four deterministic WAV variants per acoustic tag plus `default`, choosing a
  variant without an immediate repeat and applying ±5% pitch jitter;
- uses a substantially reduced base volume (-18 dB walking, -14 dB sprinting,
  -15 dB landing) with a modest 4 dB sprint increase;
- emits `footstep_event(tag, sprinting)` for headless tests;
- emits `landing_event()` after an airtime longer than 0.3 s and plays the land sample;
- falls back to the `default` set for untagged floors and logs each unknown collider
  path once.

`scripts/player.gd` and `project.godot` are untouched.

### Library-room flooring

- Added `room_catalog/module_acoustics.json`, generated by
  `scripts/module_acoustics.py` from the accepted module poetics.
- The table records each module’s acoustic tag, matched style-tag evidence, and
  the accepted poetics path/hash. Precedence is:
  `rock_field → debris`, `concrete_grey → concrete`,
  `carpet_floor/dark_carpet → carpet`, then plaster/metal/wood/tile.
- The current catalog maps:
  - carpet: kit rooms, later-bay, source-textured rooms, Bill Viola;
  - concrete: both industrial-hall modules;
  - debris: both debris/window-rock rooms.
- At assembly time, `generative_map_assembly.gd` reads the table and sets
  `surface_acoustic`, `surface_acoustic_source` and `surface_acoustic_module`
  on the instanced module’s `walk_floor_bodies`. Library scenes remain
  hash-pinned and unedited.

### Audio

`scripts/generate_footstep_audio.py` synthesises 33 mono 22.05 kHz WAVs:
four variants for each of the seven acoustic tags, four `default` variants and
one `land` sample. They are 170–280 ms. The revised `footsteps-0.2.0` synthesis
uses soft filtered-noise heel and sole impacts with a very small material tick;
the tonal sine component is removed and a final 2 kHz low-pass removes harsh
high-frequency noise. The samples peak at 0.50. `audio/footsteps/provenance.json`
records the generator, profiles, seed rule, byte counts and SHA-256 values. No
downloaded audio was used. Measured spectral centroids are 300–658 Hz, with
less than 1% of energy above 4 kHz.

The optional room-volume reverb send was not implemented; it remains off by
default as allowed by the issue.

The test verifies:

- no footsteps while standing still;
- a walking sequence across carpet, concrete, debris (library-tagged) and an
  untagged default section, in that order;
- walk stride count is within two events of distance / 0.7 m;
- sprinting produces fewer events than walking over the same distance and marks
  the events as sprinting;
- exactly one landing event after a scripted fall, with no airborne footsteps;
- every placed library floor collider carries the acoustic tag projected from
  `module_acoustics.json`.

## Deviations and review points

- The optional reverb bus is omitted.
- The acoustic table uses style-tag precedence; unknown modules remain untagged
  and deliberately fall back to `default` with one log line per collider path.
- Footstep variant/pitch randomness is session-random, not seeded per run; the
  no-immediate-repeat rule and ±5% jitter are enforced.
- Audio output was not asserted, only the emitted events, as required.
- No library scene, `player.gd`, `project.godot`, generated map, or unrelated
  workspace change was modified.

Protected inputs remain untouched. #200 was not closed and no commit was made.
