### The column-orbit finding

The capture gate **rejects the very clip that historically produced the accepted
`corridor_splat` asset**. The 8 s column orbit scores only 0.17 frame widths of
camera travel (an orbit translates less than it rotates), so the gate's
`camera_path_too_short` block fires. The older `preflight_parallax.py` gate this
clip passed was calibrated to a 6° triangulation angle and did not test net
translation; the new gate (#90) does. Both gates are internally consistent; the
pipeline's Stage A is now stricter than the historical front-end, so the existing
shipped asset is not reproducible through the current one-command pipeline.

This is a bounded, evidence-backed outcome, not a failure of the asset: the
existing splat, collision, contract and walk remain valid as the historical
artefacts they are.
