# Issue 264: materials for open-world props

## Problem and evidence

The snow-garden v2 room's accepted-reference record calls `stone_robe.png` a `stone_bench_surface`, but records its origin as a direct crop of frame 4347, bounds `[200, 280, 380, 470]` (xyxy), without resize or color edit. The crop plan and manifest preserve those bounds and the frame and crop hashes. The room script loads that crop as `materials.stone` and applies it to the bench and statue plinth. The #255 report identifies the pixels as the angel's robe, not bench stone, and says the panorama only glimpses the bench; its articulation and placement are authored. A crop's provenance therefore does not establish that it depicts the material assigned to a prop. [Sources: `godot_walk/source_library/sources/video-d92fc1fa764aa3a2/rooms/snow-garden-courtyard-v2/adaptation_standalone_accepted_v1.json`, `godot_walk/source_library/sources/video-d92fc1fa764aa3a2/rooms/snow-garden-courtyard-v2/crop_plan.json`, `godot_walk/source_library/sources/video-d92fc1fa764aa3a2/rooms/snow-garden-courtyard-v2/textures/crop_manifest.json`, `godot_walk/source_library/sources/video-d92fc1fa764aa3a2/rooms/snow-garden-courtyard-v2/room.gd`, `godot_walk/evidence/2026-11-17-issue-255-room-candidate/REPORT.md` §6.]

The #255 report describes the candidate at report time as pending review; the later standalone record marks it `accepted_reference`. This is a reference acceptance, not evidence of calibrated registration or scale: that record and the v4 freeze both keep `registration_accepted` and `scale_calibrated` false. The standalone record references the v3 freeze. Preserve the existing accepted artifacts and frozen files; a future material experiment needs a new candidate and explicit review, not an in-place rewrite of this record. [Sources: `godot_walk/evidence/2026-11-17-issue-255-room-candidate/REPORT.md` §§6–7; `godot_walk/source_library/sources/video-d92fc1fa764aa3a2/rooms/snow-garden-courtyard-v2/adaptation_standalone_accepted_v1.json`; `godot_walk/room_catalog/contract_freeze_v3.json`; `godot_walk/room_catalog/contract_freeze_v4.json`.]

The library keeps outputs source-local and distinguishes operator approval and catalog admission from linking an immutable candidate and decision; decision records also have ledger reconciliation requirements. Those are constraints for a later production workflow, not authorization to register this experiment. [Source: `godot_walk/source_library/README.md`.]

## Options

Costs below are relative estimates, not measured timings. Visual fidelity and suitability require operator judgment; automated checks can establish provenance and consistency, not semantic truth.

1. **Direct source prop crop.** Inspect existing direct-source frames for a sufficiently visible, unobstructed surface of the same prop; record the frame hash, bounds and extraction operation, then use that crop in a new candidate. Cost: low if suitable pixels already exist; potentially high manual review if they do not. Risk: perspective, lighting, low resolution or occlusion can make even a genuine prop crop misleading or unusable; a crop from a different object must not be labeled as bench pixels. Agent: check paths, hashes, bounds and reproducibility, and capture the candidate if the environment permits. Operator: decide whether the pixels actually depict the intended surface and whether the result is faithful.
2. **Explicit project-authored material fallback.** Use a locally authored solid-color or simple project material for an uncertain prop, recording its authored origin, intended material class, parameters, assignment and rationale separately from source observations. The existing candidate already uses local solid colors for surfaces absent from its seed; this proposal extends that distinction to props. Cost: low authoring and recordkeeping, plus visual review. Risk: can look generic or imply unsupported stone detail; avoid calling it a source reconstruction. Agent: verify the declared parameters, assignments, dependency paths and unchanged source artifacts. Operator: judge whether it reads as plausible stone and fits the scene. [Sources: `godot_walk/evidence/2026-11-17-issue-255-room-candidate/REPORT.md` §2; `godot_walk/source_library/sources/video-d92fc1fa764aa3a2/rooms/snow-garden-courtyard-v2/room.gd`.]
3. **Curated reusable material asset.** Build a small, explicitly licensed project material library and assign a suitable stone asset to a new candidate, with asset identity, license, provenance and assignment recorded. Cost: medium to acquire or author, review, maintain and document assets. Risk: licensing or source mismatch, and a generic texture can falsely suggest observed details. Agent: check that declared files, hashes, assignments and license records exist; operator: approve rights/suitability and visual fit. No asset availability or license has been established in this research.
4. **Multi-view surface reconstruction.** If appropriate source views exist, investigate deriving a texture from multiple views and retain a traceable transformation record in a new candidate. Cost: high capture review, tooling and compute; no model or GPU run is authorized by this research. Risk: insufficient coverage, registration artifacts and baked lighting; reconstructed appearance could be mistaken for calibrated geometry. Agent: verify input/output hashes and reproducibility if a future authorized process exists; operator: judge visual fidelity and approve any compute expenditure. Specific tool capabilities and resource requirements are unverified.

## Recommendation

Start with option 2 as an explicitly authored fallback, while recording whether option 1 has adequate direct evidence. It avoids assigning unrelated robe pixels to a bench and works even when source coverage is inadequate, without claiming source-faithful stone. Keep the material decision separate from geometry, source observations, registration and scale. Option 1 may replace the fallback in a later candidate only after the source pixels and suitability are reviewed; a reusable library or reconstruction can be evaluated separately when justified.

### Ready-to-file first implementation slice

**Title:** Record and preview an authored prop material fallback for the snow-garden bench

**Scope:** Create a new, isolated candidate derived from the snow-garden reference, with an authored stone-like bench material and a machine-readable material decision alongside it. Record the bench's material origin as `project_authored`, its explicit color/parameters, intended role, rationale, and the fact that the available reference does not establish a faithful bench surface. Identify the original reference and its hashes; distinguish any source crop evidence from the authored fallback. Restrict the visual change to bench parts: the reference shares its stone material with the statue plinth, so give the bench a separate assignment rather than silently changing that plinth. Do not change the accepted room or frozen artifacts, catalog or ledger, and keep both calibration flags false. No model, GPU, downloads or paid calls. This is a preview for review, not acceptance or registration. ## Unknowns not tested

- Which authored color or texture will read as stone in the actual scene, or whether the operator will approve it; no material or visual experiment was run.
- Whether a suitable reusable material exists locally, its license, and its compatibility with this scene; no asset or rights audit was performed.
- Whether multi-view reconstruction is feasible for this source, or its tool, compute and quality requirements; no reconstruction, model, GPU or download was run.
- Whether a new candidate and its proposed material record satisfy the current validators or can be captured in this environment; no candidate was implemented or tested. No registration or scale calibration was attempted.
