# Issue #256: outdoor and open-world ingestion research

## Evidence and problem

The frozen v3 room contract and its pinned validator are identified by `godot_walk/room_catalog/contract_freeze_v3.json`; `godot_walk/room_catalog/video_adaptation_contract_v3.md` and `godot_walk/scripts/video_adaptation_polygon.py` describe and check a finite, simple, orthogonal XZ walkable polygon. The closed perimeter is a room boundary; socket approach and clearance rules apply where connections are declared (`godot_walk/room_catalog/video_adaptation_contract_v3.md`). A polygon can represent an L-shaped walkable footprint, but cannot describe an infinite terrain, curved canyon walls, arbitrary slopes, or topology beyond that footprint. An open sky is already possible: a closed *horizontal* perimeter does not require a visible ceiling (`godot_walk/evidence/2026-11-17-issue-172-room-candidate/REPORT.md`).

The accepted standalone canyon candidate is an authored L-shaped courtyard with six closed edges and no sockets, using the panorama as sky; the report explicitly calls it a bounded stand-in, not a reconstruction (`godot_walk/source_library/sources/video-22909eb734e4d8e2/rooms/slot-canyon-courtyard-v1/adaptation_standalone_accepted_v1.json`, `godot_walk/evidence/2026-11-17-issue-172-room-candidate/REPORT.md`). The accepted standalone snow-garden v2 likewise has a closed 20×16 uncalibrated footprint, authored wall ring and static panorama sky; the report distinguishes its then-pending review from later acceptance (`godot_walk/evidence/2026-11-17-issue-255-room-candidate/REPORT.md`, `godot_walk/source_library/sources/video-d92fc1fa764aa3a2/rooms/snow-garden-courtyard-v2/adaptation_standalone_accepted_v1.json`). The report describes the source as an open park and the walls as invented traversal bounds. Acceptance of these stand-ins does not establish open-world fidelity or catalog admission.

The source library supports video and picture-set seeds, retains provenance and operator gates, and explicitly makes no claim about hidden geometry or connectivity (`godot_walk/source_library/README.md`). A monocular panorama supplies directional color at one viewpoint, not measured depth, scale, occluded surfaces, traversability or parallax; a larger image alone cannot resolve those ambiguities. The canyon report treats its panorama as visual sky, not geometry or a metric map (`godot_walk/evidence/2026-11-17-issue-172-room-candidate/REPORT.md`). This is an inference about the information available, not a reconstruction accuracy measurement.

The local runner's documented default is 512×256 (`godot_walk/evidence/2026-11-15-issue-171-hunyuan/ATTEMPT-2-REPORT.md`). A local audit records two failed 768×384 requests and successful 512×256 outputs, alongside failures and a RAM preflight block even at 512×256 (`godot_walk/evidence/2026-11-13-issue-210/REPORT.md`). The canyon report describes blurred 512×256 previews (`godot_walk/evidence/2026-11-16-issue-172-hunyuan/REPORT.md`). This evidence does not establish the highest feasible size, the cause of every failure, or current free GPU/RAM. Reports of other #251/#253 failures have not been independently verified here. No external tool or paper capability is asserted as verified in this research.

## Options

1. **Keep the room contract; improve outdoor presentation.** Author an open sky, distant visual scenery and source-derived details within a finite enclosed collision footprint, explicitly labeling invisible or naturalistic bounds. Cost: low; current room authoring and validation remain applicable. Risk: players still encounter an invented edge and curved terrain remains approximated; scenery can imply unreachable paths. An agent can validate the bundle, provenance, collision and boundary tests; only the operator can judge whether the outdoor impression and limits are acceptable. Existing precedent: `godot_walk/evidence/2026-11-17-issue-255-room-candidate/REPORT.md`.

2. **Category and separate bounded outdoor contract.** Add an operator-reviewed `enclosed` / `open_world` classification at intake, with an explicit unknown/needs-review state. Route enclosed sources to the existing frozen room contract; define a separately versioned outdoor variant with a finite playable region, explicit boundary behavior and collision, open-sky/distant-visual declarations, and observed/authored/unknown provenance. A finite region can have soft visual boundaries or explicit exits; it must not claim to be infinite. Cost: medium, including schemas, validators, migration and fixtures. Risk: category errors, misleading scenery, and compatibility with room-based assembly; avoid silently changing the freeze or admitting outdoor records into the room catalog. An agent can test routing, rejection, backward compatibility and safety invariants on CPU fixtures; the operator must review classification, source fidelity and whether boundary behavior feels natural.

3. **Connected outdoor chunks with explicit transitions.** Build on option 2 with multiple finite terrain regions, explicit edge/portal transitions, seam rules, streaming and collision ownership. This could support an apparently extensive traversable landscape, not a proof of actual unboundedness. Cost: high: new representation, generation, assembly, streaming and end-to-end traversal tests. Risk: seams, gaps, invented connectivity, resource use and accumulation of reconstruction error. An agent can check deterministic adjacency, connectivity and collision on fixtures; the operator must judge visual continuity, source fidelity and whether transitions feel open. The source library's warning against inferred connectivity remains relevant (`godot_walk/source_library/README.md`).

4. **More evidence and resolution.** Preserve multiple source views and seek a separately authorized, guarded resolution experiment, comparing 512×256 with a larger supported candidate and recording configuration, peak resources, failures and visual results. Additional views may constrain geometry, but do not guarantee recovered depth or scale. Cost: capture/review work and potentially expensive GPU/RAM runs; risk: memory exhaustion and inconsistent or hallucinated views. An agent can audit receipts and verify dimensions, provenance and resource logs; the operator must authorize runs and judge visual improvement and source fidelity. The existing guarded workflow and review gates are documented in `godot_walk/source_library/README.md`. Any proposed external reconstruction or upscaling tools, their compatibility and resource requirements are **unverified** here.

## Recommendation

Choose option 2, initially as a classification and contract proposal, and retain option 1 as an explicitly bounded fallback. It addresses the mismatch before expensive generation, preserves existing enclosed behavior and makes the distinction between visual horizon and safe walkable area reviewable. Defer chunk streaming and any claim of infinite space until a single outdoor region and its boundary have operator acceptance. Investigate resolution separately: the existing 768×384 failures are evidence for caution, not a measured hardware ceiling. Preserve the source library's operator decisions and provenance (`godot_walk/source_library/README.md`).

### Ready-to-file first implementation slice

**Title:** Add reviewed scene category and draft bounded outdoor ingestion contract

**Scope:** Add a versioned intake category (`enclosed`, `open_world`, `unknown`) for video and picture sets, with explicit operator review and provenance. Existing records with no category must remain readable and route to the existing enclosed workflow without rewriting historical records. Define a separate, versioned outdoor candidate contract for a finite playable area, safe spawn and collision, boundary/exit semantics, open-sky and visual-only horizon, plus observed/authored/unknown evidence. Keep outdoor candidates out of existing room catalog admission until a later integration design. No GPU calls, model runs, automatic classification, streaming, terrain reconstruction or edits to frozen artifacts in this slice.

**Acceptance:** Category and review state are validated and auditable; unknown or unreviewed sources cannot silently enter the outdoor path. Enclosed and legacy records retain existing validation and routing. Outdoor fixtures distinguish finite collision bounds from visual horizon, reject missing boundary or spawn declarations, and preserve uncertain scale/connectivity. A contract document specifies how exits and intentional boundaries are represented without implying unobserved destinations. No outdoor fixture is presented as an accepted real reconstruction.

**Verification:** Run focused CPU tests for valid and invalid categories, legacy records, review gates, contract variants, invalid bounds/spawn, and catalog exclusion, plus the relevant existing ingestion and contract regression suite. Check fixtures and reports for provenance and no changes to frozen hashes. Operator review remains necessary for category choices, boundary experience and source fidelity; test results alone are not acceptance of a real scene.

## Unknowns not tested

- Current GPU model, free VRAM/RAM, maximum successful Hunyuan resolution, failure causes for every historical attempt, and quality/resource tradeoffs above 512×256; no GPU or model run was performed.
- Whether a larger panorama improves canyon or snow-garden traversal or fidelity, or whether a different capture, model, depth method, or upscaler would help; external capabilities and papers are unverified.
- Real outdoor dimensions, elevations, hidden geometry, walkable extent and connectivity of the sources; the cited candidates use authored, uncalibrated geometry.
- Whether proposed categories, collision boundaries, transition semantics, or visual horizon satisfy the operator; no implementation, new capture, runtime test, or operator review was performed for this proposal.
- Integration and performance of outdoor chunks, streaming, terrain collision and sky handling in the existing assembly; these are design options, not established capabilities.
